The Image Library

156 photographs. Every tile is numbered. Call one out by its number and we'll rename it, move it, or prune it.

How to change any of this. Open gallery-data.json in a text editor. Set "keep": false to prune a photo, edit "title" to rename it, or change "cat" to move it. Then run python3 build-gallery.py again. Your edits are merged, never overwritten, and new photos get added on the end.
